After the Rain

A Second Chance Coastal Romance

By Bruno Becker and Jairo Alencar

Some places call you home. Some people give you a reason to stay.

Caio returns to Porto Sereno expecting a short stay, but rebuilding the family inn brings him face to face with Miguel and unfinished feelings. Through storms, family complications, and difficult conversations, both men must decide whether a second chance can become a lasting choice.

An emotional contemporary LGBTQ+ romance about belonging, rebuilding a life, forgiveness, and choosing love even when leaving would be easier.

What kind of book is it?

A character-driven second-chance romance with two adult male leads, coastal and small-town atmosphere, emotional history, and found-family themes.

This is the English-language edition of Depois da Chuva, the first book in the Corações da Costa / Coastal Hearts literary collection.
